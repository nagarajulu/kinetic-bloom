## 3. Master Visual Architecture & Style Specification for Engineering & Image-Generation Agents

### 3.1 Recommended Dual-Level Visual Asset Strategy

To make the **`[ 📷 Photo ]`** mode in both `PathwayHeroVisual.tsx` and `StepIllustration.tsx` clinically accurate, engaging, and cohesive with the existing main reflex illustrations, we recommend a **two-tier illustration architecture**:

1. **Option A (Recommended Best-in-Class — 51 Multi-Panel Pathway Storyboard Sheets + Step Quadrant Cropping):**
   - Generate **51 4-Panel (or 3-Panel) Clinical Storyboard Sheets** (`/images/pathways/<pathway_id>.jpg`), modeled directly after the existing [`public/images/moro_transient_steps.jpg`](file:///Users/naga.aerakoni/workspace/projects/kinetic-bloom/public/images/moro_transient_steps.jpg) (2x2 grid for 4-step pathways, or 3-panel + overview card in a 2x2 grid for 3-step pathways).
   - **Why this is ideal:**
     - Setting `pathway.transientSequenceImageUrl = "/images/pathways/<pathway_id>.jpg"` and `pathway.imageUrl = "/images/pathways/<pathway_id>.jpg"` immediately populates the full 4-panel progression banner in `ReflexLibrary.tsx` AND gives `PathwayHeroVisual.tsx` a pathway-specific illustrated photo.
     - Even better: in `StepIllustration.tsx` and `PathwayHeroVisual.tsx`, when a step uses a 2x2 quadrant sheet (`panelIndex: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4`), a simple CSS `object-position` / `scale(2)` crop (or pre-sliced `/images/steps/<pathway_id>_step_<n>.jpg` crops via `sharp` / `sips` / `canvas`) gives **every single one of the 156 steps its own distinct, dedicated step illustration** from just 51 generated master sheets!
2. **Option B (Direct Individual Step Generation — 51 Pathway Heroes + 156 Step Illustrations):**
   - Alternatively, generate `/images/pathways/<pathway_id>.jpg` for each of the 51 pathways and `/images/steps/<pathway_id>_step<n>.jpg` for each of the 156 steps.
   - **Hybrid Pipeline Recommendation:** Generate the **51 2x2 Quad Storyboard Sheets** (`/images/pathways/<pathway_id>_sheet.jpg`) using `image-generator` so that character clothing, room lighting, and equipment stay **100% visually consistent across all steps of a pathway**, and then run a deterministic Node/Python crop script to slice each 2x2 sheet into 4 individual step images (`/images/steps/<pathway_id>_step1.jpg`, `step2.jpg`, `step3.jpg`, `step4.jpg`)! This guarantees zero character drift between Step 1, Step 2, Step 3, and Step 4 while producing both the full sequence sheet AND individual step photos.
